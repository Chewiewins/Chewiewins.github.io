Three pillars

Keep it simple. These three do most of the work.

🥗

Nutrition

Calories in vs out decides weight. Get enough protein (about 1.6 g per kg), plenty of fibre, more unsaturated fats and less saturated fat, sugar and salt.

💪

Exercise

Move every day and lift something a few times a week. Strength keeps muscle while you lose fat; walking adds steady calorie burn.

🧘

Stress & Psychology

Sleep enough, keep stress down, and be consistent. A small deficit you can stick to beats a big one you quit.
